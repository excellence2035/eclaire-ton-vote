## Why INFO (rule 5 of the run brief)
- Only two forces can realistically stand on separate lists in 2027: the PDGE-led "democratic coalition" (PDGE + 14 allied parties, BTI 2026) and CPDS, which BTI 2026 calls "the only legal opposition party".
- PCSD (Buenaventura Monsuy Asumu) ran for president in 2022 (0.68%) but was allied with the PDGE for the legislative and municipal polls (Wikipedia 2022; AFP via Seneweb, 6 Nov 2022: opposition considers him a token candidate). No published policy positions found.
- Ciudadanos por la Innovación (CI): dissolved by the courts in 2018; leader Gabriel Nsé Obiang Obono arrested 29 Sep 2022 with 150+ supporters (Amnesty 2022/23), sentenced in 2023 (BTI: life sentence / 29 years, the report is inconsistent), still imprisoned per BTI 2026. Cannot run.
- Exiled groups (MLGE3R; Nexos-GE platform launched Aug 2023 by Juan Carlos Ondo Angue) boycott elections (BTI 2026). They do not contest.
- CPDS itself: its website shows mostly undated historical pieces; no 2025–2026 policy statement found in 14 searches in Spanish, French and English. Latest dated positions: Esono's 2022 denunciation of fraud (EFE). So even the second force cannot be coded on most themes.

## Election status (verified)
- No date set. Last Chamber/Senate/municipal elections: 20 Nov 2022 (with the presidential), previous: 12 Nov 2017. Five-year legislature → next due by Nov 2027.
- IPU Parline, Senate 2022 page (FETCHED by prior researchers): "Next election: expected 30 Nov 2027". 55 elected + 15 appointed by the President; 74 senators per Feb 2023 update (ex officio). Turnout 98.4% (413,148 / 419,817). PDGE and allies won all 55 seats. Presidential vote brought forward 5 months to coincide, citing costs. Obiang 97% (official), Esono 2.3%. Govt arrested opposition activists before polls. First session 19 Jan 2023. https://data.ipu.org/parliament/GQ/GQ-UC01/election/GQ-UC01-E20221120
- Wikipedia 2027 page (FETCHED prior): "November 2027 or earlier"; Chamber 100 seats, closed-list PR in 19 districts, 10% threshold; Senate 70 (55 elected same way, 15 appointed). PDGE 100/100 & 55/55. PM Manuel Osa Nsue Nsua. 2017 decree dissolved Chamber, Senate and municipal councils together. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Equatorial_Guinean_parliamentary_election
- Wikipedia 2022 page (FETCHED): presidential, parliamentary and local on 20 Nov 2022; Parliament merged them in Sept 2022 citing economic constraints; CEN confirmed 3 presidential candidates 14 Oct; PCSD allied with PDGE for legislative and municipal; PDGE won all municipal seats; official presidential: Obiang 405,910 (97.00%), Esono 9,684 (2.31%), Monsuy 2,855 (0.68%); turnout 98.33% of 427,671; US "serious doubts". Seats by district table (Malabo 10/6, Bata 10/6, Annobón 3/1 …). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Equatorial_Guinean_general_election
- Presidential term: 7 years (BTI: general elections every seven years since 1989; 2011 amendment two 7-year terms); next presidential ~2029 — not part of 2027 vote.
- Municipal councils: elected with the Chamber in 2017 and 2022; this edition assumes (not confirmed) they will be renewed alongside in 2027.

## Rights context
- Freedom House 2026 (FETCHED prior): Not Free, 5/100 (PR 0/40, CL 5/60). "holds regular elections, but voting is neither free nor fair"; frequent detention of opposition politicians; judiciary under presidential control. https://freedomhouse.org/country/equatorial-guinea/freedom-world/2026
- BTI 2026 (FETCHED this run): CPDS only legal opposition party; state funding; ~5,000 paying members; stopped publishing La Verdad 2021; BTI questions its independence ("may collude"). 14 allied parties in "democratic coalition"; coalition won 55 Senate, 100 Chamber, 588 municipal seats in 2022. CEN headed by the interior minister (ruling party); state media access for candidates "effectively banned". PDGE 35% women candidate quota. CI banned 2018; Gabriel Nsé life sentence 2023 / 29 years military court (inconsistent). Exiled coalitions boycott elections; Nexos-GE (Aug 2023). Annobón: 2024 environmental protests → mass arrests, transfers to Malabo prisons; exiles symbolically declared independence 8 Jul 2022. Somos+ activist Joaquín Elo Ayeto arrested 1 Aug 2024. Anti-corruption commission (2020; Teodorín led it to Nov 2024; Tomás Esono Ava since). Oil exports −6%/yr since 2012; ExxonMobil left 2024. Pressure for handover to Teodorín. https://bti-project.org/en/reports/country-report/GNQ
- Amnesty Report 2022/23 via ecoi.net (FETCHED): 29 Sep 2022 police arrested Gabriel Nse Obiang Obono and 150+ supporters at CI HQ in Malabo; 4 (official) / 9 (party) deaths; 119 released 6 Oct; he remained detained. CI dissolved 2018. https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2089514.html
- AFP via Seneweb 6 Nov 2022 (FETCHED): CPDS "one of only two opposition movements allowed to field candidates and the only opposition party not banned"; PCSD allied with PDGE in past votes, seen by opposition as token candidate; arrests campaign; govt accused Spain/France/US diplomats of interference for attending CPDS rally. https://www.seneweb.com/en/news/Afrique/la-guinee-equatoriale-accuse-madrid-paris-et-washington-d-ingerence_n_392429.html
- EFE via Swissinfo 27 Nov 2022 (FETCHED): Esono (from Madrid): "Todo es una chapuza"; officials spent "seis días intentando hacer cuadrar los números"; had warned of "fraude monumental". https://www.swissinfo.ch/spa/la-oposici%c3%b3n-denuncia-el-resultado-electoral-en-guinea-ecuatorial/48090114
