Public officers must resign by 9 Feb 2027; party membership lists 16 Mar 2027; party primaries and intra-party disputes done by 9 May 2027; independents must not be party members by 9 May 2027; nominations 29 May–11 Jun 2027; campaign 29 May–7 Aug 2027.
