## Uncertainties
- National Assembly date: IEC calendar (Oct 2025) and Wikipedia say **10 Apr 2027**; NDI (Aug 2026) loosely says "March 2027" (nominations/campaign start in March). Coded 10 Apr 2027 exact.
- Presidential nominations: IEC calendar 26 Oct–6 Nov 2026; NDI says deadline 5 Nov. Text only says "open on 26 October".
- NUP appears in both Coalition 2026 (The Point, 10 Aug) and Team Gambia (Foroyaa, 30 Sep) lists; the CHANGE coalition (APeX, Aug 2026) seems superseded.
- Barrow's legal basis for a third term: the 1997 Constitution has no term limit (2020 and 2024 drafts with limits both failed).
- Meet the People Tour budget figures (D53m 2025, D52m 2026) come from Darboe, not from a budget document.
