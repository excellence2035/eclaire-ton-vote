## Transition facts
- 25 Sep 2025 protests over water/power cuts; 12–13 Oct CAPSAT joined; Rajoelina fled on French military aircraft; National Assembly impeached him 14 Oct (130 votes, 1 blank); HCC decision 14 Oct entrusted Randrianirina; sworn in 17 Oct 2025 as President of the Refondation. Senate leadership replaced 12 Oct. ConstitutionNet (Ramasy) 3 Nov 2025 https://constitutionnet.org/news/voices/refondation-et-transition-constitutionnelle-madagascar (FETCHED). AU suspended Madagascar.
- First PM Herintsalama Rajaonarivelo; his first decree stripped Rajoelina of Malagasy nationality. Midi via AllAfrica 26 Jan 2026 "Huit candidats potentiels" https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601260133.html (FETCHED). Potential candidates: Randrianirina (no party base), Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko (Assembly speaker), Ravalomanana (TIM), Hajo Andrianainarivelo, Fanirisoa Ernaivo (Justice Minister), Tahina Razafinjoelina, Antoine Rajerison, Parisoa Andriambolanarivo. MAPAR could field substitute if Rajoelina barred.
- New government announced 25 Mar 2026, continuity; Gen Z (Herizo Andriamanantena) says youth have no say; NGO Ivorary (Harnelle Rakotobe) wants ministers' asset declarations published. RFI via AllAfrica 27 Mar 2026 https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202603270171.html (FETCHED)
- 2026 finance law (Midi via AllAfrica 20 Nov 2025): "sobriété budgétaire"; energy 646 bn Ar (renewables + JIRAMA recovery); water 140 bn; health 89 bn + 500 posts; primary/secondary education 75 bn + 9,000 teaching posts; higher ed 34.5 bn + 100 posts; roads 440 bn; growth 4.8%; inflation 7.1%; VAT 30.9% of tax revenue. https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202511200559.html (FETCHED)
- Gen Z arrests April 2026: 4 Gen Z members arrested after 11 Apr protest at place du 13 Mai (against power cuts, water cuts, corruption); 2 more charged with "atteinte à la sûreté de l'État"; Amnesty: "libérer immédiatement". - Gen Z march Apr 2026: demand dissolution of National Assembly, CENI, HCC; constituent assembly; people's committee at Fokonolona level. Randrianirina said politicians and foreign money finance them. L'Express Apr 2026 https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/gouvernance-de-la-transition-la-gen-z.html (FETCHED)
