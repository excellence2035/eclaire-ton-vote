## DECISION: INFO page, not a questionnaire
- On 8 Oct 2026 the transitional PM (Ilídio Vieira Té) said the 6 Dec vote must be postponed and the CNE said it cannot technically be held; no candidacy was filed at the Supreme Court by the 6 Oct deadline; most party leaderships have lapsed (no congresses).
- Political activities/meetings still restricted (Oct 2026); opposition offices closed; PAIGC leader DSP in military detention since 10 Jul 2026; PRS split; Embaló barred from returning and possibly caught by the new 5-year residence rule.
- One search for 2026 party programmes (cashew, fisheries, etc.) found none. 2025 campaign material exists, but the field of contenders for the next vote is unknown, so a 30–40 question comparison across 3+ parties could not be sourced honestly.
- META: general, 2026-12-06, q exact (decree still formally in force), with a note on the announced postponement. Re-check for a new date and switch META/stripDate when the President announces it.

## Background known before research (to verify, do not cite until opened)
- 23 Nov 2025: presidential + legislative elections. PAIGC/Domingos Simões Pereira excluded from presidential race; PAI–Terra Ranka coalition list rejected for legislative. Fernando Dias main challenger to Embaló.
- 26 Nov 2025: military takeover before results; Gen. Horta N’Tam (Inta-A) sworn in as transitional president (one-year transition announced). PM: Ilídio Vieira Té.
- ECOWAS suspended Guinea-Bissau.
